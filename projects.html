Project 1

Generative AI Web Application

In this project I designed and developed a custom website that integrated client-server communication to handle AI requests and display responses. The application I created was a College Football Playbook Decider for The College Football 26 video game. The user could select different form elements that described the type of offense they wanted to run and the AI would generate playbook suggestions that fit the criteria.

I was able to customize the look and feel of the website as well as create an application that I actually used. It was a fun and interssting way to learn more about creating websites.

For this project I used HTML, CSS, and JavaScript to create a form that gathers information from the user about what type of offense they would like to run in College Football 26.

I used different CSS stylings to make my webpage look like a coach's clipboard. I also found a font that looked like a dry-erase marker and used that for the text on the website to drive home the aesthetic.

I used JavaScript to send the user's selections to a server that I set up using Node.js and Express. The server would handle the request and send it to the OpenAI API, which would generate a response based on the user's input. The server would then send the AI's response back to the client, where it would be displayed on the website.

Project 2

Interactive Comic: Quantum Entanglement

For this project I worked with two of my classmates to create an interactive 2D comic in Unity that explained the concept of quantum entanglement. One of the main goals of the comic was to simplify a complex quantum mechanic principle in a way that a general audience could understand. We created an original story that included three puzzles that the player must solve that demonstrate differnt aspects of quantum entanglement.

This was a difficult project that required a depth of research and time, but I enjoyed the challenge of the creative process and implementing our team's design. I was especially glad to have the opportunity to worldbuild and design a story for our comic, as that is one of the aspects of game design that I enjoy the most. I also got to practice and use multiple different skills and tools including Adobe Photoshop and Animate, as well as Unity and writing scripts in C#.

video of comic? just my panels?

This project took place in three main stages: research, art, and development. First, as a team we researched our topic, Quantum Entanglement, and brainstromed ideas for how we could demonstrate the concept in a simple, understandable way.

I had the idea for our story to follow a scientist/adventurer character who discvoers a temple on an unexplored planet. Inside the temple would be puzzles that demonstrate aspects of Quantum Entanglement.

This evolved into the final version of our story in which an astronaut/doctor is mysteriously called to a planet where he finds a seemingly abandoned facility. Upon entering, he finds himself trapped inside and must solve three puzzles using Quantum Entanglement in order to escape. Once all three puzzles have been completed, and using pieces of machinery awarded along the way, he is able to complete a machine: the Quantum Communicator. He discovers that the puzzles had been set up by himself from an alternate dimension in an effort to be the richest, and most important, person in the multiverse.

Our team then created a storyboard and script for our comic and decided to finish the art for our panels first. I made the artwork for my panels and puzzle in Adobe Photoshop

The next step was to add our content to Unity and create the interactive elements for our comic. My puzzle was based on the spin up/spin dwon experiment in which when one entangled particle is observed to be spinning up, the other is now known to be spinning down. I made a controller that has five spinners on it. Those spinners are each entangled with a spinner on the lock of a vault. The player must set the spinners to the correct position to open the vault and complete the puzzle.

One script flips the animation of the spinners when they are clicked. A lock combo script checks to see if the combination is correct after each flip. Once the combo is correct, the vault can be opened and the final panel of the comic page is revealed.

I also made the final animation for our comic that explains that the player has been using Quantum Entanglement to solve the puzzles and provides the ending of the story. I drew the backgrounds in Photoshop and then used the puppet pin tool in After Effectsand rotoscope techniques in Animate.

Project 3

Escape the Dark Castle

In this project my classmate and I were tasked with creating a complete playable level in Unreal Engine 5 that included an AI entity, user interface, and a win/loss condition. We decided to model our game off of the board game "Escape the Dark Castle" and created a level where the player must navigate a level of a dungeon by solving puzzles and defeating enemies.

This was a fun and fulfilling project and I enjoyed getting to draw inspiration from a game that I love. It was a great opportunity to learn more about Unreal Engine and to practice my level design and implementation. I got to learn about and expeince new things like Unreal's AI Behavior system and Blueprint scripting.

Our game consisted of a trapped dungeon maze with three different rooms, or encounters, within the dungeon maze. The first was a combat room in which you had to defeat a small group of enemies and save an npc in order to progress further into the maze.

The second room held a pit that the player had to cross using an invisible bridge. The player is told, through the game's written dialogue, that only by faith may they cross. Additionally, we placed skeletons in the pit on either side of the bridge, but not in the middle, as an extra hint to the player.

The third room was a final combat encounter where the player faced off against a larger group of enemies and, if successful, opened a door to escape the dungeon.

One of my tasks was the implementation of the combat system, including the creation of enemy AI behaviors and the player's combat mechanics.

I created the AI behaviors for the enemies using Unreal Engine's built-in framework, including an AI Controller and Behavior Tree.

I used downloaded assets for the player's combat animations and sword. I set up the animations so that they worked with the sword and player model. I also created the hit detection script which used sphere tracing to detect when the player's sword made contact with an enemy.
